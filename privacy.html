What we collect, and why

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Short version: Without an account, Antares processes the Solana contract addresses you look at on supported trading sites, the technical metadata that any HTTPS service inherently sees (IP, browser user-agent), a random install identifier, and a history of the scans made with it. If you create an account or buy a plan, we also process your e-mail address and the record of your purchase. We do not collect your name, your wallet or private keys, or your browsing history beyond the page where an address is detected. No analytics, no advertising, no data sale. You can delete your account yourself from your account page.

1. Overview

Antares (“the Extension”) is a browser extension that scans Solana token contract addresses on supported trading platforms and returns a risk verdict. This page explains what we process, the third parties involved, and the rights you can exercise. The canonical source is the in-extension policy at antares-extension.vercel.app/privacy.html — this page mirrors it for visitors who haven’t installed the Extension yet.

2. Who we are

The Extension is operated by the COMEALAMAISONGROUPE project. Bug reports and feature requests go through the support page. For privacy questions, see section 12.

3. Data we process

When the Extension detects a Solana contract address (base58, 32–44 characters) on a supported page, that address is sent to our API at antares-extension.vercel.app/api/scan. The address is public blockchain data. Our legal basis for processing it is legitimate interest — it is the minimum data required to deliver the verdict you explicitly requested.

Any HTTPS request to our API is received by Vercel’s edge network. Vercel sees and may briefly log:

  • your IP address (used for rate limiting; not stored beyond a 60-second sliding window in Upstash Redis)
  • your user-agent string (browser identifier)
  • the contract address sent in the query string

We do not use this metadata to identify you, for analytics, for profiling, or for advertising. In our own logs, an e-mail address is never written in clear: only a short one-way reference appears.

Install identifier. If the Extension sends an X-Antares-Install header (a random opaque identifier generated by your browser on first install), we use it together with your network address as a rate-limit key so multiple users sharing a corporate NAT each get a fair quota. On its own the identifier says nothing about you. It is also the key under which we keep your scan history and, if you have a plan, the plan attached to your install. If you sign in to an account or redeem a licence, the identifier is linked to your e-mail address. You can see it on the Extension’s Options page, and you can start again with a new one by uninstalling and reinstalling the Extension.

Scan history. For each scan made with an install identifier we record the contract address, the token’s symbol and name, the verdict and score, and the time of the scan. We keep about the 1,000 most recent scans per install. The history view only shows the last 30 days, but older entries are not erased at 30 days: they stay until newer scans push them out, or until the data is deleted (section 9).

Account (optional). You only need an account to use a paid plan across your devices. If you create one we store your e-mail address, your password as a salted one-way hash (we cannot read your password), the date of creation, a random account identifier and a session counter. We use them to sign you in and to give you access to the licences bought with your e-mail. Legal basis: performance of the contract between you and us. When you sign in, our API sets one cookie, antares_session (HttpOnly, valid 30 days), which holds a signed session token; the website also hands a copy to the Extension, which keeps it in its own storage so the overlay can show your plan. Signing out ends the session in that browser. To protect accounts against password guessing we keep, for 15 minutes, a counter of failed sign-ins under a one-way hash of the e-mail address (legitimate interest: security).

Purchases (optional). Plans are paid in cryptocurrency through NOWPayments. When you start a checkout we create a checkout record: a reference, the plan, the price, the e-mail you gave, your install identifier, and the payment identifiers and transaction hash that NOWPayments reports. The checkout record is deleted automatically after 7 days. We send NOWPayments the price, our order reference and a description; we do not send your e-mail address. NOWPayments collects what it needs to process the payment, under its own policy. When a payment is confirmed we create a licence record: the licence key, your e-mail address, the plan, the amount, the dates, the payment reference and, once you redeem the licence, the install identifier that redeemed it. This is the accounting record of the sale: we keep it for as long as the law requires us to keep accounting records, and it is not erased when you delete your account. Legal basis: performance of the contract and legal obligation.

What we do not collect: your name, phone number or postal address; wallet addresses or private keys (the Extension never connects to your wallet); your full browsing history; advertising or analytics trackers. The Extension itself sets no cookies; the only cookie is the sign-in cookie described above.

4. Where the Extension activates

The Extension’s content script runs only on the trading platforms declared in the manifest’s host_permissions. On any other site the Extension is dormant. As of this version:

  • dexscreener.com
  • pump.fun
  • axiom.trade
  • photon-sol.tinyastro.io
  • birdeye.so
  • www.geckoterminal.com
  • gmgn.ai
  • app.telemetry.io

On these sites the Extension reads the URL and the visible page DOM to find a Solana contract address. It does not read form fields, credentials, cookies, or other page content.

6. International transfers

Our infrastructure (Vercel, Upstash, Sentry) operates primarily from data centres in the United States. If you access the Extension from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, technical request metadata and, if you use an account, your account data are transferred outside your region. We rely on the processors’ Standard Contractual Clauses where available. Each processor’s transfer mechanism is documented in their privacy policy linked above.

7. Data retention

  • Scan cache: 20 seconds to 30 minutes (asymmetric per verdict; risky tokens cache longer for safety, safe-looking young tokens cache shorter to avoid stale-clean errors). Keyed on contract address only, never on your IP or install-id. For up to 6 hours we also keep, per token, the last good answer of some of our data sources, and use it only when that source is down.
  • Rate-limit windows: sliding windows of 10 and 60 seconds keyed on (IP, install-id). Keys auto-expire.
  • Scan history: about the 1,000 most recent scans per install identifier, until you delete your account or ask us to delete it.
  • Account: until you delete it. Sign-in sessions last 30 days at most; deleting your account ends all of them immediately.
  • Failed sign-in counter: 15 minutes.
  • Checkout records: 7 days.
  • Purchase records (licences): for as long as the law requires us to keep accounting records.
  • Error telemetry (Sentry): 90 days. Captures stack traces and request IDs only — never the contract address, your IP or your e-mail.
  • Vercel access logs: Vercel’s default retention applies (typically 30 days). We do not download or process these logs.

8. Chrome permissions

The Extension requests the following permissions, declared in package.json:

  • storage — persists your settings (theme, alert thresholds) locally in your browser. Never synced to a server.
  • notifications — lets the Extension fire a Chrome notification when a previously-scanned token’s verdict escalates to DANGER or RUG.
  • activeTab + tabs — lets the Extension read the URL and the visible page DOM on supported sites only.
  • alarms — periodic background re-scan of tokens you’ve flagged for monitoring.

9. Your rights

Under GDPR (EEA / UK / Switzerland), CCPA (California), and similar laws, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • request correction or deletion
  • object to or restrict processing
  • data portability
  • lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority

Delete your account yourself. On your account page, choose Delete account and confirm with your password. This erases your account (e-mail address, password hash, session counter), the link between your installs and your e-mail address, and the scan history and the plan stored on those installs. Every session ends immediately, in every browser and in the Extension. What we keep is the purchase record: the licence key, your e-mail address, the plan, the amount and the dates, because the law requires us to keep accounting records. If you later create an account with the same e-mail address, your licences are available again.

Without an account. The data we hold about you is the scan history and the rate-limit counters tied to your install identifier. Copy the identifier from the Extension’s Options page and email us at the address in section 12: we delete the history held under it. Uninstalling the Extension and clearing its data removes what stays on your device.

To access your data, correct it, receive it in a portable format, or exercise any other right, email the address in section 12 with a description of the right you are exercising. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Children’s privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under 13 (US) or 16 (EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Security and disclosure

Security issues should be reported privately by emailing contact@antaresscan.com with subject [SECURITY]. Do not include exploit details in any public channel. If a data breach affects you, we will notify you without undue delay and no later than 72 hours after becoming aware of it, in line with GDPR Article 33.

12. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, email contact@antaresscan.com — see the support page for bug reports, feature requests, or verdict feedback. For security reports, email the same address with subject [SECURITY]. Do not include exploitable details in any public channel.

13. Changes to this policy

If we materially change how the Extension processes data, we will update the date at the top of this page and publish a note in the repository’s CHANGELOG.md. Continuing to use the Extension after a change means you accept the updated policy.
